## 2. The System (Four Pillars)

The Precise System™ *(working name, may change)* is the architecture. Four pillars, equal weight, closed loop.

| Pillar | Public name | Short description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Device** | Multi-wavelength pico laser engineered to execute the protocol. |
| 2 | **Protocols** | Indication-specific clinical frameworks, including the PIH Prevention Protocol™. |
| 3 | **Biologic Control** | Prep, recovery, and maintenance kits that optimize healing and outcome consistency. |
| 4 | **Data Intelligence Layer** | Real-world treatment outcomes feed back into protocol refinement. |

**The system framing line:**
> A laser is a tool. The protocol is the medicine. The system is what makes both work.

## 7. Voice Reminders for All Page Copy

- Sentence case. No Title Case headlines.
- No exclamation points.
- No emoji.
- The system is the subject. Not "we." Not "our team."
- One idea per sentence. Lead with the verb.
- ™ on first appearance per page: Precise Aesthetics™, Precise Pico™, PIH Prevention Protocol™, The Precise System™.
- Em dashes — no surrounding spaces.
